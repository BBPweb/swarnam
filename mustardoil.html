Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is mustard oil made from?

Mustard oil is extracted from mustard seeds and is widely used in traditional Indian cooking.

2. What gives mustard oil its distinctive flavour?

Its characteristic pungent flavour and aroma come from naturally occurring compounds associated with mustard seeds, particularly allyl isothiocyanate.

3. Can mustard oil be used for everyday cooking?

Mustard oil is traditionally used in various cooking preparations, including sautéing, frying and preparing regional dishes.

4. Is mustard oil used for pickle preparation?

Yes. Mustard oil is commonly used in traditional Indian pickle recipes, where its pungent flavour complements the spices and other ingredients.

5. How should mustard oil be stored?

Store it in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight and heat. Keep the container tightly closed after use to maintain its quality and freshness.
