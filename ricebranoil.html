Swarnam Rice Bran Oil

Bringing together nutritional goodness and everyday versatility, Swarnam Rice Bran Oil is a thoughtful choice for modern kitchens. Extracted from the bran of rice, this edible oil is valued for its balanced fatty acid composition and naturally occurring nutrients. With its mild flavour and versatility, it blends effortlessly into everyday cooking, making every meal a little more wholesome.

Uses of Rice Bran Oil

i.

Everyday Cooking

Its mild flavour makes rice bran oil suitable for preparing a variety of everyday dishes without overpowering the natural taste of ingredients.

ii.

Deep Frying

Its relatively high smoke point makes it suitable for deep frying, helping achieve evenly cooked and crispy preparations.

iii.

Sautéing and Stir-Frying

Works well for sautéing vegetables, stir-frying and preparing quick meals while allowing the flavours of other ingredients to stand out.

iv.

Baking and Grilling

Can be used in baking and grilling, making it a versatile addition to different cooking styles and recipes.

Benefits of Rice Bran Oil

i.

Rich in Unsaturated Fatty Acids

Contains monounsaturated and polyunsaturated fatty acids, including oleic acid and linoleic acid, which contribute to its distinctive nutritional profile.

ii.

Naturally Contains Gamma-Oryzanol

A characteristic component of rice bran oil, gamma-oryzanol is a naturally occurring group of antioxidant compounds.

iii.

Source of Vitamin E Compounds

Contains naturally occurring vitamin E compounds, including tocopherols and tocotrienols, which have antioxidant properties.

iv.

A Balanced Fatty Acid Profile

Contains a combination of saturated, monounsaturated and polyunsaturated fatty acids, making it a versatile option for everyday cooking.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is rice bran oil made from?

Rice bran oil is extracted from the outer bran layer of rice grains. It is processed and refined to produce edible cooking oil.

2. What makes rice bran oil different from other cooking oils?

Rice bran oil is known for its combination of fatty acids and naturally occurring compounds such as gamma-oryzanol, tocopherols and tocotrienols.

3. Is rice bran oil suitable for everyday cooking?

Yes. Its mild flavour and versatility make it suitable for everyday cooking methods, including sautéing, frying and stir-frying.

4. Can rice bran oil be used for deep frying?

Yes. Rice bran oil generally has a high smoke point, making it suitable for deep frying and other high-temperature cooking methods.

5. How should rice bran oil be stored?

Store it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat. Keep the container tightly closed after use to maintain its quality and freshness.
