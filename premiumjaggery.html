Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Premium Jaggery?

Premium Jaggery is a variant of traditional jaggery used as a sweetening ingredient in Indian cooking. Its specific characteristics depend on its source, grade and processing.

2. What makes Premium Jaggery different from regular jaggery?

The distinction generally depends on factors such as the raw material, processing, colour, texture and grade. The specific differences in Swarnam Premium Jaggery depend on its product specifications.

3. What can I prepare with Premium Jaggery?

It can be used to prepare traditional sweets, payasam, pradhaman, festive delicacies, beverages and other recipes that call for jaggery.

4. Can Premium Jaggery replace regular jaggery in recipes?

Yes. It can be used in most recipes that call for jaggery. The quantity can be adjusted according to the sweetness and flavour required.

5. Is Premium Jaggery suitable for everyday cooking?

Yes. It can be incorporated into everyday cooking, traditional desserts and homemade preparations according to your requirements.

6. How should Premium Jaggery be stored?

Store it in a clean, dry, airtight container in a cool place, away from moisture, direct sunlight and pests.
