Swarnam Gor Keri Pickle

A distinctive speciality in traditional Indian pickling, gor keri is known for its delicious combination of raw mango, sweetness, tanginess and spices. Popular in Gujarati cuisine, this traditional pickle brings together contrasting flavours to create a memorable taste. Swarnam Gor Keri Pickle adds a distinctive sweet-and-tangy character to your meals, making it a delightful accompaniment to everyday favourites.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is gor keri pickle?

Gor keri is a traditional Gujarati-style raw mango pickle known for its combination of sweet, tangy and spiced flavours.

2. What makes gor keri different from regular mango pickle?

Unlike many conventional mango pickles, gor keri is known for its distinctive sweet-and-tangy flavour profile, which balances the sourness of raw mango with sweetness and spices.

3. What can I eat gor keri pickle with?

It pairs well with thepla, parathas, chapatis, rice and a variety of traditional Indian meals.

4. Is gor keri pickle sweet or spicy?

Gor keri is generally known for its combination of sweetness, tanginess and spice, making it different from predominantly sour or spicy pickles.

5. Is gor keri pickle suitable for everyday meals?

Yes. It can be enjoyed as an accompaniment to everyday meals and snacks according to your preference.

6. How should gor keri pickle be stored?

Store it in a clean, dry, airtight container and use a dry, clean spoon. Follow the storage instructions on the product packaging.
