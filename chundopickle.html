Swarnam Chundo Pickle

A traditional Gujarati favourite, chundo pickle is known for its delightful combination of sweet, tangy and spiced flavours. Prepared around the distinctive taste and texture of shredded mango, this pickle offers a delicious contrast to savoury meals. Swarnam Chundo Pickle brings this regional speciality to your kitchen, adding a distinctive sweetness and tanginess to everyday dining.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is chundo pickle?

Chundo is a traditional Gujarati mango preparation made using shredded mango and known for its distinctive sweet, tangy and spiced flavour.

2. What makes Chundo different from regular mango pickle?

Chundo is typically known for its shredded mango texture and sweeter flavour profile, while many regular mango pickles are predominantly tangy and spicy.

3. What can I eat chundo pickle with?

It pairs particularly well with thepla, parathas, chapatis, rice and other traditional Indian meals.

4. Is chundo pickle sweet or sour?

Chundo is known for its balance of sweet and tangy flavours, often complemented by a mild spiced character.

5. Can chundo pickle be used as an accompaniment to snacks?

Yes. It can be enjoyed with a variety of snacks and light meals, adding a sweet and tangy flavour.

6. How should chundo pickle be stored?

Store it in a clean, dry, airtight container and use a dry, clean spoon. Follow the storage instructions on the product packaging.
