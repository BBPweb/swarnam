Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is tamarind commonly used for?

It is commonly used in sambar, rasam, fish curries, vegetable gravies, chutneys and pickles to add a tangy flavour.

2. How do I prepare tamarind extract?

Soak the required amount of tamarind in warm water until softened. Squeeze to extract the pulp and strain before adding it to your preparation.

3. Can I use tamarind in everyday cooking?

Yes. Tamarind is a versatile ingredient that can be used in a variety of everyday dishes, particularly curries, gravies and traditional recipes.

4. How should I store tamarind?

Store it in an airtight container in a cool, dry place, away from moisture.
