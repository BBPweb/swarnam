75 Years of Legacy
That Keeps Growing

Swarnam started with a single step, a small mill and a bunch of sesame sacks. It's come a long way from those early days to being in the hearts of so many people today. It is built solely on the values and promises that have underpinned it since its founding years in the 1950s. It is honest work and enthusiasm that resulted in today’s Swarnam Group.

It’s been a long journey, and we have evolved at regular intervals along the way. We built new branches and possibilities from our dream seed. It's gotten deeper with new sub-brands, categories and broader ranges. Change is a must and for Swarnam it has been about growing deeper and wider.

From the Banks of the Periyar

In the 1950s, shortly after India's independence, Hassan and Alikunju started their small venture called the Brothers Oil Mill on the banks of the Periyar River in Aluva. Modern technology, gigantic machines and marketing facilities were still a thing of the future. It was entirely dependent on hard work and aspirations.

In the 1990s, the firm made great strides in developing its sound corporate policy. Rather than diversifying, it chose to focus on oil production. In 1998, the firm entered the Kerala Market under the Swarnam trademark, making gingelly oil available due to its easy packaging. At that time, the Kerala market of gingelly oil was ruled by Tamil Nadu brands. It was very difficult to secure even a small niche for itself in this well-established market.

Three Specialised Units

Swarnam started with only one mill, and has branched out into three specialised units:

United Oil Industries

Oversees production processes including milling, refining, bottling, packaging, and delivery.

United Polymers

Manufactures custom high-quality packaging to keep products safe from the factory to stores.

United Marketing

Provides an expansive distribution network using years of knowledge in local markets.
