Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Sona Masoori Rice?

Sona Masoori is a medium-grain rice variety widely cultivated in parts of South India. It is known for its mild aroma, delicate flavour and relatively lightweight texture.

2. What makes Sona Masoori Rice different from other rice varieties?

Sona Masoori is valued for its medium-grain structure, mild flavour and versatility, making it a popular choice for everyday cooking.

3. Is Sona Masoori Rice suitable for everyday consumption?

Yes. It is commonly enjoyed as a staple in everyday meals and pairs well with a variety of Indian dishes.

4. Can Sona Masoori Rice be used for making biryani?

Yes. It can be used for certain rice preparations and regional biryani variations, although the choice of rice depends on the desired texture and recipe.

5. How should Sona Masoori Rice be cooked?

Wash the rice thoroughly and cook it with an appropriate quantity of water until the grains become tender. Water ratios and cooking times may vary depending on the variety and preferred texture.

6. How should Sona Masoori Rice be stored?

Store it in a clean, dry, airtight container in a cool place, away from moisture, direct sunlight and pests.
