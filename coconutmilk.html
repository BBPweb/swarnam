Swarnam Coconut Milk

Coconut milk brings a smooth, creamy texture and the distinctive richness of coconut to a variety of traditional and contemporary dishes. Widely used in curries, desserts and other coconut-based preparations, it adds depth and body to recipes while enhancing their flavour. Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is coconut milk used for?

Coconut milk is used in curries, gravies, traditional Kerala dishes, desserts and other recipes that call for a creamy coconut base.

2. Can I use coconut milk in curries?

Yes. It can be added to curries and gravies to give them a smooth texture and rich coconut flavour.

3. Can coconut milk be used in desserts?

Yes. It is suitable for payasam, puddings and other traditional and contemporary desserts.

4. Can I use coconut milk in Kerala recipes?

Yes. Coconut milk is an important ingredient in many Kerala preparations, including curries and traditional dishes.

5. Does coconut milk make dishes creamy?

Yes. Its smooth consistency helps add body and creaminess to a variety of preparations.

6. How should I store coconut milk?
