Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is ginger garlic paste?

Ginger garlic paste is a culinary preparation that combines ginger and garlic into a ready-to-use paste, commonly used in Indian cooking.

2. What is ginger garlic paste commonly used for?

It is widely used in curries, gravies, biryani, pulao, marinades, stir-fries and other traditional preparations.

3. Can ginger garlic paste be used for marinating?

Yes. It can be used in marinades for vegetables, meat and seafood, adding the distinctive flavours of ginger and garlic.

4. Can I use ginger garlic paste instead of fresh ginger and garlic?

Yes. It can be used as a convenient alternative to freshly prepared ginger and garlic in many recipes. Adjust the quantity according to your taste and the recipe requirements.

5. Does ginger garlic paste need to be refrigerated after opening?

Follow the storage instructions mentioned on the product packaging. Refrigeration may be required after opening, depending on the product formulation.

6. How should ginger garlic paste be stored?

Store it according to the instructions on the packaging. Always use a clean, dry spoon and close the container properly after use.
