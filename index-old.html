I.

From the banks of the Periyar.

A small mill. A bag of sesame seeds. Two brothers with no plan beyond doing honest work.

Soon after India's independence, two brothers — Hassan and Alikunju — set up a modest oil mill on the banks of the Periyar in Aluva. They called it United Flour & Oil Mill. They sold gingelly oil and flour in bulk. They built their business on one principle: never short a customer, never adulterate a product. That principle outlasted them.

The flagship · Since 1998

Most gingelly oil sold in India is a story.
Ours is a test result.

Every batch of Swarnam Nallenna is required to clear Gas Liquid Chromatography (GLC) — a laboratory technique that separates and identifies every compound in the oil. The result is binary: either the oil is pure sesame, or it is not. We do not ship a batch until it is.

We are one of the few gingelly oil brands in India that submits to this standard voluntarily.

About Swarnam Group

Opening

A small mill. A bag of sesame seeds. Two brothers with no plan beyond doing honest work.

That is genuinely how Swarnam Group began.

In 1957 — ten years after independence — Hassan and Alikunju set up a modest operation on the banks of the Periyar in Aluva. They called it United Flour & Oil Mill. They sold gingelly oil and flour, mostly to wholesalers, mostly in bulk, mostly to people they knew by name.

Sixty-eight years later, we still know our customers by name. We just have a few more of them.

The second generation.

When the business passed to the next generation, the four sons — Abdul Rahman, Faisal, Jaffer and Abdul Hameed — made one decision that defined everything since:

They went deeper, not wider.

Instead of diversifying away from oil, they doubled down. They renamed the company United Oil Industries. They concentrated almost entirely on gingelly oil. They committed to a level of purity that, at the time, no one in the Kerala market was offering at scale.

In 1998, that commitment became a brand: Swarnam Nallenna. It went on to become Kerala's most trusted gingelly oil.

Growing The Group

What started as one mill is now three companies, designed to work together:

  • United Oil Industries — Our core manufacturing arm. Where the oil is milled, refined, and bottled. Where every batch is tested before it leaves the floor.
  • United Polymers — We make our own packaging. Not because it's cheaper — because it lets us control how our products travel, sit on shelves, and reach kitchens. A bottle is part of the product.
  • United Marketing — Our distribution and field marketing arm, over three decades old. The reason a Swarnam bottle ends up in a kirana shop in Palakkad on the same week it's available in a supermarket in Kochi. This is what we mean when we say we control the chain. The seed enters our system. Everything from that point — milling, refining, bottling, branding, distributing — is us.

Rice Bran Oil · Since 2008

Helth — light oil for the way we actually cook.

Pure rice bran oil from Swarnam Group. Lighter on your food, kinder to your heart, stable enough for your hottest pan.

A word on the spelling. "Helth" is intentional. We wanted a name as direct as the product — fewer letters, no excess, nothing pretending to be more than it is. Same idea as the oil.

Why rice bran

It is the oil for people who want to deep fry without flinching.

i.

Gamma-oryzanol

A rare antioxidant, naturally present in rice bran oil and almost nowhere else, studied for cholesterol balance.

ii.

High smoke point

Stable at the temperatures most Indian cooking actually happens at. Safe for deep frying.

iii.

Less absorption

Lower viscosity means less oil per dish. Your bottle lasts longer, your food eats lighter.

iv.

Balanced fatty acids

Close to what WHO considers ideal in a cooking oil. Naturally free of trans fats.

Swarnam Coconut Oil

Kerala's oil, made the way Kerala has always made it.

Pure coconut oil from Swarnam Group. For your aviyal, your hair, your morning uzhichil — and for everything Kerala has used coconut oil for, since long before anyone called it a superfood.

In the kitchen and beyond

How Kerala actually uses coconut oil.

i.

In the kitchen

Aviyal, thoran, olan, fish curry. The entire backbone of Kerala cooking. Other oils change the dish. Coconut oil completes it.

ii.

On hair

Generations of Kerala women knew what research now confirms: coconut oil reduces protein loss in hair.

iii.

On skin

A traditional moisturizer, studied for its effect on dry skin and skin barrier function.

iv.

In ritual

From the nilavilakku to the oil for newborns, coconut oil is woven into Kerala's daily life.

We are not going to claim it cures diseases. We are going to say: it is the oil this state has cooked with for two thousand years, and that is its own kind of evidence.
