Sliced Coconut

Swarnam Sliced Coconut brings the natural goodness of coconut to everyday cooking in a convenient, ready-to-use form. Carefully sliced for ease of preparation, it can be used across a variety of traditional recipes where coconut is an essential ingredient. From curries and vegetable preparations to other familiar dishes, it adds the natural richness and texture associated with coconut.
