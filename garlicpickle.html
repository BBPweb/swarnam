Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is garlic pickle?

Garlic pickle is a traditional condiment prepared using garlic and a combination of spices and other ingredients, known for its bold and savoury flavour.

2. What does garlic pickle taste like?

It offers the distinctive flavour of garlic combined with tangy and spicy notes, creating a bold taste experience.

3. What can I eat garlic pickle with?

Garlic pickle pairs well with rice, curd rice, chapatis, parathas, dosa and other traditional meals.

4. Is garlic pickle suitable for everyday meals?

Yes. It can be enjoyed as an accompaniment to everyday meals according to your taste and preference.

5. Does garlic pickle have a strong flavour?

Yes. Garlic has a naturally distinctive flavour, which gives this pickle its characteristic bold and savoury taste.

6. How should garlic pickle be stored?

Store it in a clean, dry, airtight container and always use a dry, clean spoon. Follow the storage instructions on the packaging.
