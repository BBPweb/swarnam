Most gingelly oil sold in India is a story. Ours is a test result.

Every batch is required to clear Gas Liquid Chromatography — a laboratory technique that separates and identifies every compound in the oil. The result is binary: either pure sesame, or not. We do not ship a batch until it is.

Use cases

In a Kerala kitchen, gingelly oil is not optional.

i.

Tempering (thalippu)

A spoonful with mustard seeds and curry leaves — the foundation of half of Kerala's everyday dishes.

ii.

Pickling

The traditional carrier for naranga curry, manga curry, and most Kerala-style pickles. Stable, flavorful.

iii.

Cold dishes

Drizzled raw over rice, kanji, or steamed vegetables — the way most of central Kerala still eats it.

iv.

External use

Traditional in Ayurvedic massage (abhyanga) and as a hair oil base.

Section IV
IV.

The benefits, honestly.

We've stripped out the inflated claims. Here's what nutrition research actually supports about pure sesame oil.

i.

Heart-Friendly Fats

Sesame oil naturally contains sesamol and sesamin—plant compounds that have been studied for their antioxidant properties and their potential role in supporting healthy cholesterol levels as part of a balanced diet.

ii.

Naturally Rich in Good Fats

Predominantly made up of monounsaturated and polyunsaturated fats, with relatively low saturated fat, making it a well-balanced choice for everyday cooking.

iii.

Source of Magnesium & Zinc

Provides naturally occurring minerals including magnesium and zinc, both of which contribute to normal metabolic function and bone health.

iv.

Essential Fatty Acids

Contains linoleic acid—an essential omega-6 fatty acid that the body cannot produce on its own and must obtain through diet.

We're not going to tell you that gingelly oil cures anything. We are going to tell you that it remains one of the most studied traditional edible oils in Indian cuisine—and that we make the cleanest version of it we know how to make.

Frequently Asked Questions
VII.

Questions we get all the time.

Everything you need to know about Swarnam Nallenna, from purity and production to cooking and availability.

Is Swarnam Nallenna pure sesame oil with no additives?

Yes. Swarnam Nallenna is 100% pure cold-pressed sesame oil with no additives, blending oils, or preservatives. Every batch undergoes Gas Liquid Chromatography (GLC) testing before it is released.

What is the difference between gingelly oil and sesame oil?

There is no difference. "Gingelly oil" is simply the South Indian name for sesame oil. The source, extraction process, and product are exactly the same.

Is Swarnam Nallenna good for daily cooking?

Yes. It is ideal for everyday cooking including tempering, sautéing, pickling, and finishing dishes. For deep frying at higher temperatures, our Rice Bran Oil is the better choice.

Where is Swarnam Nallenna made?

Swarnam Nallenna is manufactured at our facility in Aluva, Kerala, by United Oil Industries, a part of Swarnam Group.

Where can I buy Swarnam Nallenna?

It is available across Kerala through supermarkets, grocery stores, and leading retailers. You can also purchase it through Amazon, Flipkart, and our partner online stores. For wholesale or export enquiries, please contact our sales team directly.
