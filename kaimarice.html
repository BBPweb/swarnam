Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Kaima Rice?

Kaima Rice is a short-grain aromatic rice variety commonly associated with Kerala's Malabar region. It is also known as Jeerakasala Rice.

2. Is Kaima Rice the same as Jeerakasala Rice?

Yes. Kaima Rice is commonly known as Jeerakasala Rice, particularly in Kerala, where it is widely used in traditional Malabar cuisine.

3. What makes Kaima Rice different from other rice varieties?

Its short grains, distinctive aroma and characteristic texture make it different from long-grain rice varieties commonly used for biryani.

4. Is Kaima Rice suitable for making biryani?

Yes. Kaima Rice is particularly popular for preparing Malabar biryani, where its aroma and texture complement the flavours of the preparation.

5. Does Kaima Rice need to be soaked before cooking?

Soaking is commonly recommended for a short period before cooking, although the duration and method may vary depending on the recipe and rice processing.

6. How should Kaima Rice be stored?

Store it in a clean, dry, airtight container in a cool place, away from moisture, direct sunlight and pests.
