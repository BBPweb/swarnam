Swarnam Palakkadan Matta Rice

Deeply rooted in Kerala's culinary heritage, Palakkadan Matta Rice is cherished for its distinctive colour, earthy flavour and wholesome character. Grown in the Palakkad region of Kerala, this traditional rice variety has been a staple in Malayali households for generations. Benefits of Palakkadan Matta Rice

i.

Naturally Distinctive Colour

Recognised for its reddish-brown appearance, which comes from the pigmented outer layers of the rice grain.

ii.

Source of Dietary Fibre

Its partially retained outer layers provide dietary fibre, contributing to a balanced diet.

iii.

Contains Essential Nutrients

Provides nutrients such as magnesium, phosphorus and B vitamins, with the nutritional content varying according to the degree of milling.

iv.

Traditional Whole-Grain Goodness

Its distinctive processing and milling characteristics help retain more of the grain's outer layers compared with highly polished white rice.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Palakkadan Matta Rice?

Palakkadan Matta Rice is a traditional rice variety associated with the Palakkad region of Kerala. It is known for its reddish-brown colour, earthy flavour and distinctive grain texture.

2. What makes Palakkadan Matta Rice different from regular white rice?

Unlike highly polished white rice, Palakkadan Matta Rice retains more of its outer grain layers, giving it its characteristic colour, texture and nutritional composition.

3. Is Palakkadan Matta Rice suitable for everyday consumption?

Yes. It is traditionally consumed as a staple rice in Kerala households and can be enjoyed as part of everyday meals.

4. How is Palakkadan Matta Rice traditionally cooked?

It is generally washed and cooked in sufficient water until the grains become tender. Cooking time and water requirements may vary depending on the rice variety and preparation method.

5. Is Palakkadan Matta Rice rich in fibre?

Compared with highly polished white rice, Matta rice generally retains more of its outer grain layers, which contribute to its dietary fibre content.

6. How should Palakkadan Matta Rice be stored?

Store it in a clean, dry, airtight container in a cool place, away from moisture, direct sunlight and pests.
