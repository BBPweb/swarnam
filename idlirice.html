Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is idli rice?

Idli rice is a type of rice commonly used in South Indian cooking, particularly for preparing idli and dosa batter.

2. What makes idli rice suitable for making idlis?

Its starch composition and cooking characteristics make it suitable for producing the soft, fluffy texture associated with traditional idlis when used in properly prepared and fermented batter.

3. Can idli rice be used for making dosa?

Yes. Idli rice is commonly used in fermented dosa batter, helping create the desired texture and consistency.

4. Does idli rice need to be soaked before grinding?

Yes. It is generally soaked before grinding to soften the grains and help achieve a smooth batter.

5. Can idli rice be used without urad dal?

Traditional idli batter usually combines idli rice with urad dal. The combination helps achieve the characteristic texture and structure of idlis.

6. How long should idli batter be fermented?

Fermentation time varies depending on the room temperature, ingredients and conditions. The batter is generally left to ferment until it becomes airy and increases in volume.

7. How should idli rice be stored?

Store it in a clean, dry, airtight container in a cool place, away from moisture, direct sunlight and pests.
