Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is plain pappadam?

Plain pappadam is a traditional Indian crispy accompaniment made using ingredients such as flour, salt and other seasonings, depending on its preparation.

2. How do I prepare plain pappadam?

Plain pappadam can generally be deep-fried in hot oil until it expands and becomes crisp. Some varieties can also be roasted over a flame or prepared using other suitable methods. Follow the instructions on the packaging.

3. What can I eat plain pappadam with?

It pairs well with rice, sambar, rasam, dal, curd rice, curries and other traditional Indian dishes.

4. Is plain pappadam suitable for Kerala sadya?

Yes. Plain pappadam is a familiar accompaniment to Kerala sadya and other traditional festive meals.

5. Can plain pappadam be roasted instead of fried?

Depending on the product formulation, pappadam can be roasted over a flame or prepared using other suitable methods. Check the preparation instructions on the packaging.

6. How should plain pappadam be stored?

Store it in a clean, dry, airtight container in a cool place, away from moisture and direct sunlight, to maintain its crispness.
