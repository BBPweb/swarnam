Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is sliced coconut used for?

Sliced coconut is used in curries, vegetable preparations, thoran and other traditional recipes that call for coconut pieces.

2. Can I use sliced coconut in everyday cooking?

Yes. It can be included in everyday curries, stir-fries and a variety of home-cooked dishes.

3. Can sliced coconut be used in thoran?

Yes. It can be added to thoran and other vegetable preparations to provide coconut flavour and texture.

4. Does sliced coconut need to be cut before use?

No. It comes pre-sliced and is ready to use, helping reduce preparation time.

5. Can I use sliced coconut in traditional Kerala recipes?

Yes. It is suitable for many traditional Kerala preparations that use sliced coconut as an ingredient.

6. How should I store sliced coconut?

Follow the storage instructions provided on the product packaging to maintain its quality.
