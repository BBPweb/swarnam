Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is roasted rice powder made from?

Roasted rice powder is made from rice that is processed into flour and roasted to develop its characteristic texture and flavour.

2. What makes roasted rice powder different from regular rice flour?

Roasted rice powder undergoes an additional roasting process, which changes its flavour, moisture content and cooking characteristics compared with unroasted rice flour.

3. What can be prepared using roasted rice powder?

It can be used to prepare traditional dishes such as pathiri, idiyappam, puttu and various rice-based snacks.

4. How should roasted rice powder be stored?

Store it in an airtight container in a cool, dry place, away from moisture and direct sunlight. Use a clean, dry spoon when handling the flour.
