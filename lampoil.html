Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is lamp oil used for?

Lamp oil is primarily used for lighting traditional oil lamps during daily prayers, religious ceremonies and festive occasions.

2. Can Swarnam Lamp Oil be used for daily prayers?

Yes. It is intended for lighting traditional lamps during everyday devotional practices.

3. Can lamp oil be used during festivals?

Yes. Lamp oil is commonly used during festivals, celebrations and religious occasions that involve traditional lamp lighting.

4. How should lamp oil be stored?

Store it in a tightly closed container in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight and sources of heat. Keep it out of reach of children.

5. Can lamp oil be used for cooking?

No. Lamp oil is intended for lighting lamps and should not be used for cooking or consumption.
