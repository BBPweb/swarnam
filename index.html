Our story

From the banks
of the Periyar

A small mill. A bag of sesame seeds. Two brothers with no plan beyond doing honest work.

Swarnam began in Aluva, Kerala, as a modest oil mill run by two brothers who believed in honest work and better food for every home. From sesame to a trusted range of products, our journey has always been guided by one principle — never compromise on quality.
