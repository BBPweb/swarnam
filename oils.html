Swarnam Gingelly Oil

Swarnam Gingelly Oil is a timeless favourite rooted in the traditions of Indian cooking. Carefully positioned as a trusted kitchen staple, Swarnam Gingelly Oil continues to bring the taste and aroma associated with traditional cooking to modern homes, making every preparation feel a little more authentic and familiar.

Swarnam Rice Bran Oil

First introduced as Helth Oil, Swarnam Rice Bran Oil has evolved with the same commitment to quality and everyday cooking. Launched in 2008, the brand was built around rice bran oil as a versatile choice for the modern kitchen. Over the years, it has become a dependable option for a variety of everyday preparations, valued for its light texture and suitability for different cooking needs. Today, under the name Swarnam Rice Bran Oil, it continues to carry forward the same trusted product and the familiarity built with generations of consumers.

Swarnam Lamp Oil

Swarnam Lamp Oil is created for one of the most cherished traditions of the Indian home, lighting the lamp. A traditional choice for everyday prayers, festive occasions and moments of devotion, it brings warmth and light to rituals that carry deep meaning. Designed specifically for lamp-lighting, it becomes part of those quiet moments that begin or conclude the day, as well as the celebrations that bring families together. From daily prayer spaces to festive gatherings, Swarnam Lamp Oil complements these rituals with the familiarity of a trusted household brand. More than an everyday essential, it represents the simple warmth and continuity of traditions passed from one generation to the next.
