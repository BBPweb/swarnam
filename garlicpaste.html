Swarnam Garlic Paste

It adds depth to traditional recipes and forms an important part of countless everyday preparations. Swarnam Garlic Paste brings the familiar flavour of garlic to your kitchen in a convenient ready-to-use form, making everyday cooking simpler while retaining the characteristic taste of this much-loved ingredient.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is garlic paste?

Garlic paste is a ready-to-use preparation made from garlic, offering a convenient way to incorporate its distinctive flavour into everyday cooking.

2. What can I use garlic paste for?

It can be used in curries, gravies, marinades, biryani, pulao, stir-fries, sauces and other preparations that call for garlic.

3. Can garlic paste replace fresh garlic in recipes?

Yes. Garlic paste can be used as a convenient alternative to freshly chopped or crushed garlic in many recipes. Adjust the quantity according to your taste.

4. Can garlic paste be used for marinating?

Yes. It can be added to marinades for vegetables, meat and seafood, complementing other spices and seasonings.

5. Does garlic paste need to be refrigerated after opening?

Refer to the storage instructions on the product packaging. Depending on its formulation, refrigeration may be required after opening.

6. How should garlic paste be stored?

Store it according to the instructions provided on the packaging. Always use a clean, dry spoon and close the container properly after use.
