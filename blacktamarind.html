Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is black tamarind?

Black tamarind, commonly known as Kudampuli or Malabar Tamarind, is a dried fruit used as a souring ingredient in traditional Kerala cooking.

2. Is black tamarind the same as regular tamarind?

No. Both are used to add sourness to food, but they differ in flavour and culinary applications. Black tamarind has a distinctive smoky-sour taste and is particularly popular in Kerala fish curries.

3. What is black tamarind commonly used for?

It is primarily used in Kerala-style fish curries, seafood gravies and other traditional regional preparations.

4. How do I use black tamarind in cooking?

Rinse the required pieces and soak them in water before adding them to your curry. The quantity can be adjusted according to your preferred level of sourness.

5. Can black tamarind be used in vegetarian dishes?

Yes. It can also be used in selected vegetable curries and other regional preparations that call for its distinctive sour flavour.

6. How should black tamarind be stored?

Store it in a clean, dry, airtight container in a cool place, away from moisture, direct sunlight and pests.
