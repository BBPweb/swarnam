Swarnam Palakkadan Matta Rice

Swarnam Palakkadan Matta Rice is a traditional rice variety known for its rich taste, hearty texture and distinctive character. A familiar staple, it is well suited to everyday meals and traditional preparations where the natural character of the rice is an important part of the experience. Its wholesome texture makes it a dependable choice for serving alongside curries, vegetables and other accompaniments. Rooted in familiar culinary traditions, Palakkadan Matta Rice continues to hold a special place in everyday cooking. Swarnam brings this traditional variety to the modern kitchen as part of its carefully selected rice range, offering a dependable staple for those who appreciate the rich character and satisfying texture of traditional rice.

Swarnam Kaima Rice (Jeerakasala Rice)

Swarnam Kaima Rice, also known as Jeerakasala Rice, is a fragrant, fine-grained variety prized for its delicate texture and distinctive aroma. Its unique character makes it particularly suited to traditional rice dishes where fragrance and texture play an important role. Whether used in a flavourful festive preparation or a cherished traditional recipe, Kaima Rice brings an unmistakable aromatic quality to the dish. Its fine grains and distinctive character make it a valued choice for those who appreciate the finer qualities of traditional rice varieties. Swarnam Kaima Rice brings this much-loved variety to the kitchen, making it easier to create flavourful rice dishes with the familiar aroma and character associated with Jeerakasala rice.
